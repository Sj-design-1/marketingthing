About

Built for the class, not the hype.

Why we built this

To test how AI changes one creative profession, the art director, using evidence from our own room instead of predictions alone.

What we did

Made twelve ads in Adobe Firefly: two per channel, one art-directed and one from a one-line prompt. Built this page with Claude.

What we didn't do

No human-made ads are in the set, and no image was retouched after generation. The prompt is the only difference.
